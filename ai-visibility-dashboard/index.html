What the AI visibility checker looks at

AI Radar shows how visible your SaaS brand is in the conversations that large language models learn from and cite. Enter your website URL and the brand name or keyword you want to own, and the checker pulls up to 100 of the newest Reddit discussions that mention it. From those threads it reports four signals:

  • Visibility score (0 to 100): how often your keyword shows up in recent community discussion.
  • Estimated mentions: a rough read on how widely the term travels across the social web.
  • Sentiment: whether those threads lean positive, neutral, or negative, based on how people voted on them.
  • AI readiness: a plain rating of Excellent, Moderate, or Needs Work.

A low score points to a gap. Buyers are asking questions in public, and your brand isn't part of the answer yet.

Run it before you start answer engine optimization work to set a baseline, then check back monthly to see what moved.
